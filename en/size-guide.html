    Size guide

    The right size, the first time

    With a tape measure and two minutes, take your measurements and use the charts below or the size finder to pick the right size for each style. Still unsure? Size exchanges are free for 7 days.

    • Fit
    • How to measure
    • Size finder
    • Size charts

    1. Know the fit first

    Every style is cut to a fit; the fit tells you whether to take your usual size or go one up or down.

    • Slim

      Cut close to the body; if you prefer comfort, take one size up.

    • Regular

      A little room to move; choose your usual size.

    • Oversize

      Cut generously on purpose; for a neater look, try one size down.

    2. Take your measurements

    1. 1

      Chest

      Wrap the tape under your arms around the fullest part of your chest; keep it flat and parallel to the floor.

    2. 2

      Waist

      The narrowest part of your waist, just above the navel. Don't hold your breath or pull the tape too tight.

    3. 3

      Hips

      Stand with your feet together and measure the widest part of your hips.

    4. 4

      Height

      Without shoes, stand with your back to a wall and measure from the floor to the top of your head (the most important measurement for kidswear).

    5. 5

      Sleeve length

      Bend your arm slightly and measure from the shoulder point to the wrist.

    3. Size finder

    Enter your measurements in centimeters to see your recommended size.
